# Contribution and status

## Jabed's contribution

I drafted the initial questionnaire for the household-energy prediction study, structuring questions about household composition, socioeconomic circumstances, housing and occupant behaviour. This provided an initial instrument for investigating group-level differences in energy prediction.

Academic leads refined and finalised the questionnaire. The expanded revisions contain additional demographic characteristics and more detailed building, heating and occupancy questions. I describe the initial drafting as my contribution and attribute the academic refinement accordingly.
