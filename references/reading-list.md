# Reading list

This catalogue records the 16 articles in the supplied project reading folder. Titles and DOI identifiers were extracted from the PDFs. Inclusion documents the source collection; it does not imply that every article was cited in the questionnaire or that Jabed authored any of these papers.

Each entry links to the publisher record through the DOI resolver and to the original PDF retained with the research materials. Authorship and publisher rights remain with the original authors and publishers. These articles are background literature, not publications by Jabed.

## How the literature connects to the design

The energy papers cover socioeconomic, building, appliance, occupancy and behavioural variables. The study design brings those kinds of information together to formulate a particular fairness question. The proposed contribution still needs to be assessed against the wider literature and tested empirically.
